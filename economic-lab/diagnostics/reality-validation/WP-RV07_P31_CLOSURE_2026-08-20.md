## Interpretation

### A — VERIFIED EXISTING FACT

Raising transaction prices to the P30 optimistic variable-cost floor materially increases nominal sales and firm cash. Therefore the P30 underpricing diagnosis is economically active, not a purely accounting label.

However, the same intervention does not restore the economy coherently. Full-horizon unemployment and exits worsen and consumer physical output falls about `22%`.

The large increase in the nominal-budget fulfillment statistic occurs together with a near doubling of the price index and should not be interpreted as a physical-goods recovery.

### B — DIAGNOSTIC LEAD

The failure is not reducible to a single nominal price correction. Raising upstream prices shifts costs downstream, while raising consumer prices changes the quantity that household budgets can purchase. The deeper mismatch is increasingly consistent with the **physical output / productivity / input-coefficient value basis**, not merely prices being numerically too low.
