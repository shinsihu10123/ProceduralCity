## Interpretation

### A — VERIFIED EXISTING FACT

Neutralizing only the decision-time cash signal, while restoring the exact objective cash balance before credit and settlement, materially changes labor targets, unemployment and physical output.

Therefore the cognitive/decision response to cash stress is not a passive symptom. It is a causal propagation channel.

### B — DIAGNOSTIC LEAD

The same intervention causes materially larger wage arrears and late input pressure. Firms keep more labor and produce somewhat more, but the underlying cash-flow problem has not been repaired.

This sharply separates two layers:

1. **objective operating deficit / liquidity stress** — upstream economic problem;
2. **cash-stress-driven labor contraction** — major endogenous amplifier that converts the financial/operating problem into capacity loss.

The currently strongest causal loop is therefore:

`operating revenue weakness / cash deficit → cash stress → defensive or cash-preservation hiring contraction → workers/capacity ↓ → finished-goods availability ↓ → revenue weakness ↑ → distress / exit`.

P23 shows exit then adds a large late nonlinear amplifier.
