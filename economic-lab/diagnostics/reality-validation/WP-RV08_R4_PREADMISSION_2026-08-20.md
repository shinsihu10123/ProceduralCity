# WP-RV08-R4 — Coherent Repair Integration Gate (Pre-Admission)

Date: 2026-08-20
Status: **PRE-ADMISSION ONLY — AWAITS R2 RECOVERY + R3 MATRIX**

## Purpose

R4 will not return to broad diagnosis. It will combine only mechanisms already admitted by evidence into the first coherent repair candidate and test whether the remaining labor/exit feedback is financially supportable.

To increase execution throughput, R4 is planned as one wider integration matrix rather than a sequence of single-feature WPs.

## Inputs that may be admitted

### Unit basis
The RV07/RV08 structural unit-basis candidate remains the comparison foundation until a production unit-system implementation replaces the diagnostic transform.

### Working-capital timing
R1 established that current-plan input financing is a correctness repair but is insufficient by itself.

### Entrant regeneration
R3 determines whether the admitted production candidate is:
- priority startup equity,
- explicit safe-cash-gap startup equity,
- a risk-sharing/guarantee design motivated by the bank upper bound,
- or no finance candidate because a physical bootstrap boundary remains.

### Supply sequencing
R3 simultaneously determines whether topological/full-cash supply improvements remain materially complementary after entrant finance is restored.

## Planned wide R4 factorial

Once R3 selects the finance/supply base, R4 will cross it with a bounded labor/exit response rather than suppressing layoffs or exits unconditionally.

Candidate dimensions:

1. selected finance/supply base vs the corresponding control base;
2. canonical labor response vs financially supportable labor floor;
3. canonical exit response vs solvency-aware exit timing/bridge;
4. combined labor + exit response.

The labor/exit rule must use observable balance-sheet/operating feasibility. It may not preserve jobs merely to hit an unemployment target.

## Rejected shortcuts

- unconditional no-exit;
- unconditional no-layoff;
- free payroll grants;
- risk-limit tuning to obtain desired unemployment;
- using GDP improvement as the sole pass criterion;
- silently converting diagnostic upper bounds into production semantics.

## R4 success criteria

A repair bundle is eligible for canonical implementation only if it improves regeneration and macro resilience while also preserving:

- settlement and general-ledger identities;
- bank/asset-market reconciliation;
- explicit funding counterparties;
- finite firm/household states;
- physical output and inventory coherence;
- no hidden observer reverse-write;
- no fitted coefficient chosen from A/B/C unemployment paths.

Primary outcomes:

- unemployment and re-employment;
- firm exits and entrant re-exits;
- wage arrears;
- physical sector output;
- household goods fulfillment;
- intermediate-input shortage;
- entrant output/revenue survival;
- firm cash and credit/equity funding;
- bank losses/capital if risk sharing is admitted.

## Validation boundary

R4 remains candidate selection on the existing A/B/C diagnostic seeds. Held-out seeds and 120–240 month validation are reserved for the selected coherent candidate, not for every factorial arm.
