# WP-RV08 R4-CU-D3D-B5 Two-Axis Shadow Repair Family Preregistration v0.1

## Decision status

**PREREGISTERED / ACTIVATION REQUIRES B4 PASS / SHADOW ONLY / CANONICAL MUTATION NOT AUTHORIZED**

## Dependency checkpoint

B5 starts only if R4-CU-D3D-B4 confirms the preregistered severe gap on both empirical axes across Original A/C and Heldout E/F while B3 exact replay and hard-accounting gates remain healthy.

B5 does not repeat B1/B2 empirical extraction, B3 national-accounts reconstruction, or B4 gap measurement. It consumes B4 only as a scoring-band admission decision.

## Question

Can a repair family improve both (a) sectoral value-production coherence and (b) household consumption realization without destroying accounting, purchasing power, supply-chain settlement, firm survival, or deterministic replay?

## Core separation

B5 freezes two independent intervention axes.

### Axis V — value-production coherence

This axis may alter only shadow representations of the economic value generated by labour and sectoral production. Candidate mechanisms must preserve explicit sector identity and may not use one economy-wide multiplier.

Permitted mechanism families for shadow evaluation:

1. `V-PRODUCTIVITY`: sector-specific effective capacity/productivity transformation;
2. `V-BUNDLE`: sector-specific physical product-bundle reinterpretation with reciprocal unit-value accounting where required;
3. `V-RELATIVE-VALUE`: sector-specific relative product-value transformation with household purchasing-power effects measured explicitly;
4. `V-HYBRID`: bounded combinations of the above where every component is separately reported.

Blocked shortcuts:

- multiplying all sector productivity by one scalar;
- multiplying all prices by the empirical gap factor;
- dividing all wages by the empirical gap factor;
- forcing book unit cost to equal price by construction;
- erasing RESOURCE / MATERIALS / CAPITAL / CONSUMER relative-value differences.

### Axis H — household consumption-realization mechanics

This axis may alter only shadow mechanisms between disposable household resources, desired consumption planning, market execution, and realized purchaser outlay.

Permitted mechanism families for shadow evaluation:

1. `H-BUDGET-FORMATION`: bounded transformation from disposable resources and buffer state into ex-ante desired budget;
2. `H-MARKET-EXECUTION`: rationing, availability and purchaser-outlay realization mechanics without changing the empirical flow comparator definition;
3. `H-BUFFER-SAVING`: explicit cash-buffer / saving propensity mechanics that preserve household balance-sheet conservation;
4. `H-HYBRID`: bounded combinations with each component separately observable.

Blocked shortcuts:

- setting `desiredConsumptionBudget = empirical realized-consumption share × disposable income`;
- copying OECD saving-rate medians into fictional countries;
- deleting unmet demand so that realized consumption mechanically equals the empirical band;
- injecting household cash to satisfy the band;
- treating realized-consumption evidence as a direct behavioral parameter.

## Candidate construction rule

B5 candidates are mechanism combinations, not fitted answers. No numeric candidate may be generated from `model gap factor = empirical / model` or its inverse.

Candidate grids must be preregistered before observing candidate outcomes. Every candidate receives a stable ID of the form:

`B5-V{value-family}-{level}__H{household-family}-{level}`

The baseline canonical mechanism is retained as `B5-BASELINE`.

Any numeric levels introduced by the implementation must satisfy all of the following:

- derived from semantic/engineering bounds or broad log-spaced sensitivity design, not from the observed B4 gap factor;
- identical candidate grid across all four frozen seeds;
- no per-country or per-seed retuning;
- no post-hoc insertion of a level because it lands near an empirical center.

## Shadow-scoring bands

If and only if B4 passes, B5 may use the B4-admitted broad envelopes as scoring bands:

- labour-value directional envelope: B1 outer cohort-IQR `0.521557–0.607117`;
- realized household-consumption-flow directional envelope: B2 admitted outer cohort-IQR `0.883763–0.979344`.

These are scoring bands only. They are not direct canonical targets and do not authorize fictional-country assignment.

## Mandatory scorecard

Every candidate × seed must report at least:

1. positive-GVA employee-compensation-share distribution;
2. positive-disposable-income realized-consumption-share distribution;
3. non-positive-GVA and non-positive-disposable-income shares;
4. firm revenue/payroll and operating-cash recovery;
5. firm exit and entry-survival measures;
6. household nominal and real purchasing-power diagnostics;
7. desired versus realized consumption and unmet demand;
8. input-constrained firm share and procurement realization;
9. AP/AR/arrears if credit mechanics are touched;
10. employment, unemployment and wage-settlement health;
11. ledger/accounting/fiscal/international health;
12. exact deterministic replay;
13. sector-level diagnostics for RESOURCE / MATERIALS / CAPITAL / CONSUMER.

## Dominance and rejection rules

A candidate is immediately rejected if any seed shows:

- accounting or ledger health failure;
- deterministic replay failure;
- a new hidden transfer or conservation break;
- worse denominator pathology without a documented compensating mechanism;
- household purchasing-power collapse caused by blind repricing;
- supply-chain improvement financed only by unpayable arrears;
- firm survival created only by persistent subsidy/credit loss;
- material deterioration on one empirical axis while improving the other.

A candidate may enter the non-dominated set only if it improves both admitted empirical-axis distances relative to baseline on Original and Heldout seeds and does not trigger a hard rejection rule.

## Success criterion

B5 does not require landing inside both empirical envelopes. Its purpose is to identify whether at least one mechanism family produces a stable Pareto improvement on both axes while preserving the real-economy gates.

Possible decisions:

- `PASS_NONDOMINATED_TWO_AXIS_SHADOW_FAMILY_FOUND`;
- `PASS_DIRECTIONAL_IMPROVEMENT_BUT_NO_EMPIRICAL_BAND_ENTRY`;
- `FAIL_VALUE_AXIS_ONLY`;
- `FAIL_HOUSEHOLD_AXIS_ONLY`;
- `FAIL_REAL_ECONOMY_SIDE_EFFECTS`;
- `FAIL_NO_ROBUST_TWO_AXIS_IMPROVEMENT`.

None authorizes canonical mutation.
