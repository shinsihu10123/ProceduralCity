# WP-RV07-P24 — Firm Hiring-Change Driver Audit

## Purpose

P10-P12 establish that recursive labor-demand formation is causally important, while P14-P15 show that blanket labor retention is not a coherent repair. P24 moves one level upstream and identifies **why the cognitive firm decision selects negative hiring changes**.

## Observation points

Read-only snapshots are taken:

1. immediately after `firmDecision` has set `currentPlan`, `lastTrace`, price and `desiredWorkers`, intercepted just before credit origination;
2. after labor clearing and production planning;
3. after physical production.

## Exact fields

For every active firm/month:

- selected plan
- hiringChange
- exact desiredWorkers equation reconciliation
- workers before and after labor market
- cashStress
- inventoryPressure
- supplyStress
- debtBurden
- expectedDemandGrowth / observedDemandGrowth
- selected-plan utility margin
- capacity-bound state
- actual layoff execution
- output / desired production

Results are grouped by scale, window, industry and selected strategy.

## Questions

- Are negative labor targets mainly generated by `방어`, `현금 보존`, or another strategy?
- Is cash stress, inventory pressure, supply stress, debt burden, or demand belief most associated with negative hiring decisions?
- How often do negative hiring decisions occur while the firm is already capacity-bound?
- How often do those decisions execute actual layoffs?

## Hard gates

Exact observer non-interference, health, complete coverage, desired-worker equation reconciliation, matched decision/plan/production snapshots, ledger integrity, GDP identity and finite rows.

## Boundary

P24 identifies proximate cognitive/decision drivers; association is not itself causal proof of the underlying stress variable. No canonical changes, tuning, or repair authorization.
