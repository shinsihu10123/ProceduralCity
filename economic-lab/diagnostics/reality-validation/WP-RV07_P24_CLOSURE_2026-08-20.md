## Additional source-timing defect — supplyStress is structurally zero at firm decision time

The frozen monthly order calls `supply.beginMonth(country)` before the firm-decision loop. `beginMonth` resets each firm's `supplyShortage` to zero. `firmDecision` then computes `supplyStress` from that reset field.

P24 empirically records mean decision-time supplyStress of exactly `0` in every displayed window.

Therefore, under the current ordering, the cognitive firm decision cannot use the previous month's realized input shortage through this `supplyStress` channel even though the reasoning model contains such a term.

This is a **verified signal-timing defect**, but its macro causal importance is not established by P24 alone.

## B — DIAGNOSTIC LEADS

1. The late labor-capacity contraction is much more tightly associated with **cash stress** than with expected-demand decline.
2. By M10-12, negative hiring persists despite strongly negative inventory pressure, meaning firms want inventory replenishment but choose cash preservation and reduce labor anyway.
3. This creates a coherent candidate feedback:

`quantity/output loss → revenue weakness → cash stress → cash-preservation labor contraction → lower capacity → more quantity loss → distress/exit`.

4. The missing lagged supply-shortage signal may further distort decisions because firms cannot condition their current strategy on the shortage channel implemented in the reasoning model.
