## Claim classification

### A — VERIFIED EXISTING FACT

1. Payroll liquidity failure is widespread under the unit-basis candidate.
2. The failure is usually already present before same-month input procurement.
3. Procurement spending is therefore not the primary creator of payroll shortfalls.
4. Canonical credit repairs only a small fraction of otherwise unaffordable payroll cases.
5. Labor contraction rarely converts an unaffordable payroll bill into an affordable one.

### B — DIAGNOSTIC LEAD

The remaining dominant path is upstream of procurement: opening cash, debt service, prior-period operating revenue, credit origination, and the timing gap between payroll settlement and later same-month downstream sales.

### C — HYPOTHESES TO TEST NEXT

- H-P18: firm debt service materially contributes to pre-payroll cash insufficiency.
