## Causal decomposition

### 1. Exit propagation is the largest single unemployment amplifier

On the CONSUMER base, suppressing exits alone reduces FULL unemployment by about `11.27 pp` (`0.2161 -> 0.1035`).

On MATERIALS+CONSUMER, suppressing exits alone reduces FULL unemployment by about `11.08 pp` (`0.2238 -> 0.1130`).

The effect is strongly back-loaded. In M10-12 the no-exit axis reduces unemployment by roughly `28.3 pp` on CONSUMER and `25.8 pp` on MATERIALS+CONSUMER.

Therefore exit is not the initial trigger, but it is the dominant late-stage labor-destruction propagation mechanism after the objective production/cash-flow defects have already weakened firms.

### 2. Planned labor contraction is a real but smaller amplifier

No-layoff alone reduces FULL unemployment by about `3.09 pp` on CONSUMER and `4.22 pp` on MATERIALS+CONSUMER.

This confirms the earlier P24/P25 evidence that defensive/cash-preservation decisions transmit firm stress into lower employment and capacity. However, no-layoff alone does not remove the collapse and materially increases arrears.

### 3. Labor and exit interact nonlinearly

Suppressing both planned layoffs and exits collapses unemployment to approximately `1.3-1.4%` in the 12-month upper-bound runs. The joint-supply axis changes throughput and fulfillment but is not required for the large employment recovery.

This means most of the residual unemployment after productivity normalization is generated by the endogenous propagation loop rather than by an immutable labor-supply or matching ceiling.

### 4. Blanket suppression is financially invalid

The same variants that collapse unemployment produce very large wage arrears:

- CONSUMER canonical supply + no layoffs + no exits: `89976`
- CONSUMER joint supply + no layoffs + no exits: `87869`
- MATERIALS+CONSUMER canonical supply + no layoffs + no exits: `90408`
- MATERIALS+CONSUMER joint supply + no layoffs + no exits: `94621`

Therefore layoffs and exits are partly absorbing real operating deficits. Preventing them without fixing those deficits merely converts unemployment/exit into unpaid wage liabilities.
