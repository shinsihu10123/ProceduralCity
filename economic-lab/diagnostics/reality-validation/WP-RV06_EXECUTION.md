## Questions

1. Does the credit contraction precede, coincide with, or follow the real-side labor/goods deterioration already identified in WP-RV03?
2. Are rejected applications primarily caused by commercial-bank capital constraints, borrower affordability, estimated-default-risk limits, or the bank agent's counterfactual choice?
3. How much requested credit is actually supplied, by month and collapse window?
4. When do missed debt service, defaults and charge-offs begin relative to unemployment acceleration and firm exits?
5. Does central-bank liquidity support resolve reserve stress while leaving bank equity/capital constraints intact?
6. Is `lastMonetary.creditStress` a contemporaneous measure or a lagged transformation of the prior month's `lastCredit` state?

## Read-only instrumentation

The diagnostic wraps existing methods but never re-runs an economic decision:

- `BankSystem.buildApplications` — copies the exact application list generated by the frozen model;
- `BankSystem.originateCredit` — records pre/post loan IDs only;
- `AccountingSystem.entityStatement` during origination — copies the bank balance sheet passed to each credit decision;
- `BankSystem.capByBankCapital` — records whether an already-approved decision reaches the capital-cap stage and the amount returned;
- `bank.lastTrace` assignment — copies each application decision's already-produced compact reason/constraint trace;
- `BankSystem.serviceDebt` — copies exact due-state and post-service loan/payment/default state.

A separate exact non-interference control compares an unobserved and observed 3-month run before the promoted panel.

## Hard gates

- exact observer non-interference;
- v0.10 health PASS for all promoted seeds;
- complete 3 × 4 × 12 country-month coverage;
- application/approval/rejection/new-credit observer totals reconcile to `lastCredit`;
- debt payment/miss/default observer totals reconcile to `lastCredit`;
- the bank capital-safety formula matches each decision trace;
- no unexplained failure after a decision has already passed into the capital-cap stage;
- monthly `lastMonetary.creditStress` exactly reconciles to the frozen central-bank formula applied to the previous month's `lastCredit`.

## Scope caution

WP-RV06 diagnoses coded transmission and timing. It does not assert empirical calibration of default probabilities, risk limits, policy rates, capital ratios or lending standards. It does not authorize loosening credit, recapitalizing banks, changing central-bank policy, forgiving debt or changing any parameter.
