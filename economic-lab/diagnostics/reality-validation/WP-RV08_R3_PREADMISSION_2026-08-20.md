# WP-RV08 R3 — Entrant Regeneration Institution Pre-Admission Contract

Date: 2026-08-20
Status: **PRE-ADMISSION READY — WAITING ON R2 RESULT**

## Purpose

Prepare the next repair-isolation step without pre-committing to a mechanism before WP-RV08 R2 identifies which commercial-bank constraints are actually decisive.

R3 is not another broad root-cause search. It is a repair-architecture discriminator.

## Frozen requirements

- Preserve explicit settlement and general-ledger accounting.
- Preserve household/firm/bank/government balance-sheet identities.
- No fitted unemployment/GDP target.
- No unrecorded cash or physical inventory injection.
- Do not waive incumbent credit risk merely to improve macro outcomes.
- Replacement entrant treatment may differ only through an explicit lifecycle contract/institution whose funding and risk-bearing party are recorded.
- Current-plan payroll and input needs must be measurable before funding decisions.

## R2-dependent admission branches

### Branch A — targeted bank startup contract

Admit only if R2 shows that relaxing a bounded subset of ordinary-bank hard constraints materially restores entrant credit, production and revenue while bank accounting remains valid.

Required R3 comparison:
1. canonical control;
2. current-plan timing only;
3. entrant lifecycle underwriting with the R2-supported constraint treatment;
4. same treatment + explicit current-plan input requirement;
5. same treatment + one-sector-at-a-time analysis for RESOURCE/MATERIALS/CAPITAL/CONSUMER.

Promotion requires that the contract is expressed in observable borrower state, not a hidden `if entrant then approve` override.

### Branch B — explicit guarantee / risk-sharing institution

Admit if bank-capital or loss-bearing capacity is a decisive blocker even where the entrant has an otherwise financeable current plan.

Required accounting boundary:
- bank still originates the loan;
- guarantor records a contingent/guarantee exposure;
- bank capital treatment changes only through an explicit guaranteed exposure;
- realized default/loss, if any, is allocated between bank and guarantor through journaled settlement;
- guarantee cannot create net money except through the already-defined authorized lending/settlement channel.

Diagnostic variants should compare no guarantee vs partial/full risk-bearing upper bounds before choosing any production specification. No empirical guarantee percentage may be fitted from unemployment.

### Branch C — early-stage equity / startup capital institution

Admit if R2 shows that ordinary commercial-bank underwriting remains structurally closed to zero-resource entrants even after the relevant hard constraints are isolated.

Required accounting boundary:
- investor/fund cash decreases when entrant cash increases;
- firm paid-in capital/equity increases by the same amount;
- no loan liability is created for pure equity funding;
- investor records the corresponding financial asset;
- funded amount is derived from an explicit startup operating plan rather than a target macro outcome.

Candidate funding sources may include the existing household/equity-market architecture or a separately accounted early-stage fund, but R3 must not invent a source-less endowment.

### Branch D — production bootstrap after credit success

Admit if R2 obtains entrant credit but downstream production/revenue still fail.

Then test whether the remaining blockade is:
- input procurement timing,
- absence of capital stock,
- labor acquisition,
- supplier availability,
- or sales realization.

Do not continue relaxing underwriting if credit is no longer the binding stage.

## Core R3 outcome metrics

At minimum, by entrant cohort and sector:
- birth count;
- funding application/approval/origination;
- funding amount and source;
- first hire month;
- first input purchase month;
- first output month;
- first revenue month;
- re-exit share;
- 1/3/6-month survival;
- labor absorbed from exit-displaced worker pool where observable;
- outstanding liability or investor exposure;
- bank capital ratio and losses;
- guarantor/fund cash/equity/exposure if applicable.

Macro outcomes remain secondary diagnostics:
- unemployment;
- exits/entries;
- wage arrears;
- goods fulfillment;
- input shortage;
- physical sector output;
- firm cash;
- credit stress.

## Hard gates

1. deterministic replay;
2. exact control non-interference;
3. complete scale/seed coverage;
4. ledger country reconciliation;
5. general accounting equation reconciliation;
6. bank loan/deposit reconciliation where debt is used;
7. investor/fund/guarantor reconciliation where applicable;
8. no negative or non-finite physical stocks from the intervention;
9. every entrant funding receipt has a traceable counterparty/source;
10. intervention activates in the intended entrant cohorts;
11. production/revenue recovery, if claimed, must be observed physically and not inferred from book inventory/GDP alone.

## Stop rule

R3 stops once one institution family restores a non-trivial entrant production/revenue path while preserving accounting and without requiring blanket incumbent bailouts. At that point the project advances to a coherent combined repair candidate rather than expanding the matrix indefinitely.

## Authorization

This file authorizes no canonical implementation by itself. R2 remains the immediate dependency for choosing Branch A/B/C/D.
