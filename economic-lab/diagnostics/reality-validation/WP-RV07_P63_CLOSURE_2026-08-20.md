## Sector localization

Compared with consumer-only, adding MATERIALS productivity raises MATERIALS planned layoffs from `347` to `450`; the all-noncapital variant records `452`. This is a large gross labor-flow change despite stronger physical throughput.

Negative-hiring firm inventory pressure also shifts materially:

- consumer-only MATERIALS: `-0.244`
- materials+consumer MATERIALS: `+0.234`
- all-noncapital MATERIALS: `+0.347`

In the all-noncapital variant, negative-hiring inventory pressure is positive across all four sectors in the audited sample:

- RESOURCE: `+0.617`
- MATERIALS: `+0.347`
- CAPITAL: `+0.570`
- CONSUMER: `+0.347`

At the same time, MATERIALS negative-hiring cash stress rises from `0.772` under consumer-only to about `0.854` when MATERIALS productivity is normalized. Thus the observed labor penalty is consistent with throughput outrunning absorption while cash stress remains binding.
