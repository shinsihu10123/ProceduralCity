## B — DIAGNOSTIC LEADS

1. The employment recovery channel is not monotonic in aggregate productive throughput.
2. CONSUMER productivity directly relieves the seller-availability bottleneck and is the strongest real-side employment lever found in this factorial.
3. Adding upstream productivity increases intermediate throughput and often improves goods clearing/cash, but it also changes input demand, sectoral cash flows, firm decisions and labor demand in ways that can offset employment recovery.
4. MATERIALS+CONSUMER is a particularly informative interaction: better exits, arrears, output, fulfillment and cash than CONSUMER-only, yet higher unemployment. This isolates a residual labor-demand/allocation mechanism that is not reducible to firm insolvency or goods scarcity alone.
