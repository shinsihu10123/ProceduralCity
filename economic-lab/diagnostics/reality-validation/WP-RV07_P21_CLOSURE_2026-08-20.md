## Interpretation

### A — VERIFIED EXISTING FACT

From M4 onward the goods market normally exhausts all eligible consumer sellers by the end of clearing. The dominant recorded stop is `no eligible seller`, not the three-round search limit and not settlement failure.

The near equality between inventory-value / desired-budget coverage and realized fulfillment in M4-12 is also consistent with the available consumer stock being almost fully absorbed.

Household desired budgets remain materially positive throughout the collapse. Household income weakens, especially late, but the immediate market-clearing failure is not that households universally lack positive expenditure budgets.

### B — DIAGNOSTIC LEAD

The direct goods-market bottleneck is therefore upstream of household clearing: consumer finished-goods availability, which depends on production capacity, active firm base, intermediate-input supply and the labor/financial conditions that sustain those producers.

Household funding deterioration can still amplify aggregate demand over time, but it is **secondary to physical seller exhaustion at the observed clearing stop from M4 onward**.
