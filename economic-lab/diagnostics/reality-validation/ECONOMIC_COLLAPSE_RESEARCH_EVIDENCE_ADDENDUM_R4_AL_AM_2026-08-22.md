## R4-AM — revenue-supported staffing envelope

The same observer-only run shows a real interior between current staffing and the full physical labor requirement.

In `ramp-grace`:

- trailing 3-month mean realized contribution supports current employment in 73.3% of CONSUMER observations and 69.9% of M+C observations;
- trailing 3-month minimum contribution supports current employment in only 47.8% and 58.4% respectively;
- the trailing mean supports the full physical labor requirement in only 6.7% and 13.1%;
- a strict interior expansion zone — more labor than current employment but less than the full physical requirement — exists in 21.5% and 24.6% of observations.

Conclusion: the evidence rejects both unrestricted physical staffing and a one-month hard affordability cap. The dependency-safe frontier is now a smoothed, bounded, trailing-realization staffing-envelope ablation.
