## Interpretation

### A — VERIFIED EXISTING FACT

Firm deactivation is causally important once distress exits begin. Preventing it preserves employers and production capacity and strongly improves late unemployment, output and household-goods fulfillment.

### B — DIAGNOSTIC LEAD

The intervention has no M1-3 effect and only a small M4-6 output effect. Therefore exit cannot be the initial root shock. It is a **large nonlinear late amplifier** of an earlier intensive-margin / financial-operating deterioration.

The no-exit world also accumulates substantially more wage arrears and input demand/shortage. Surviving distressed firms remain economically unhealthy. This confirms that suppressing deactivation treats the propagation mechanism, not the upstream distress source.
