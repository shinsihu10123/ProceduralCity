## Exact diagnostic definition

Optimistic variable-cost floor per unit:

`wage * workers / capacity + inputPerOutput * current upstream supplier price`

This is intentionally favorable to the firm: it assumes capacity is fully utilized and excludes finance cost, taxes, depreciation, fixed cost, unsold output and working-capital cost. Therefore price below this floor is a strong internal unit-economics failure.
