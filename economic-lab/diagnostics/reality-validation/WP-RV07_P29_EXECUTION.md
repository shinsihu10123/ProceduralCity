# WP-RV07-P29 — Exact Firm Cash-Flow Waterfall and Operating-Coverage Audit

## Purpose

P25/P27 show that cash-stress cognition materially amplifies labor contraction, but suppressing that response converts part of the failure into arrears. P29 moves one level upstream and identifies **which cash-flow components actually create the objective firm cash deficit**.

## Method

Read-only audit on the unit-basis diagnostic control. For every beginning-of-month firm, use exact ledger postings to reconcile opening cash to closing cash and classify cash movements into:

- consumer / B2B / capital / government sales inflow;
- wage outflow;
- input-purchase outflow;
- debt service;
- new credit;
- capital investment outflow;
- taxes;
- residual other flow.

Core operating cash coverage is defined diagnostically as:

`sales cash / (wage cash out + input-purchase cash out)`

This is a cash-flow diagnostic, not a national-accounting profit definition.

## Questions

- Which industries are structurally below 1.0 core cash coverage?
- Does the operating deficit predate debt service and firm exits?
- Are credit and debt service large enough to explain the cash-stress path?
- Does capital investment materially drain otherwise viable firms?
- When does cash stress become persistent by sector?

## Hard gates

Exact observer non-interference, health, ledger integrity, exact firm cash reconciliation, GDP identity and finite rows.

## Boundary

No mechanism changes, no tuning, no repair authorization, no empirical-realism claim.
