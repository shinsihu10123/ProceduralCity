# WP-RV08 R4-BF–BK — Economic Ecosystem Lifecycle Wide Sweep

Date: 2026-08-23
Mode: DIAGNOSTIC / OBSERVATIONAL / NON-CANONICAL

## Purpose

Increase diagnostic breadth without multiplying redundant simulations. One 48-month instrumented run per seed will audit six structural-realism axes simultaneously under the established MATERIALS+CONSUMER normalization scaffold.

No canonical repair is authorized by this WP.

## R4-BF — Entrant lifecycle / replacement quality

Track every post-month-0 entrant as a cohort:
- birth month and industry
- birth cash / capital / inventory / workers
- first hire
- first capital acquisition
- first credit
- first output
- first revenue
- exit month
- survival to 3 / 6 / 12 operational months
- share becoming productive or revenue-positive within 3 / 6 months

Hypothesis BF-1: replacement firms are born materially under-endowed relative to incumbents and fail to replace lost productive capacity quickly enough.

## R4-BG — Circular-flow ownership / profit distribution

Measure:
- aggregate firm profit
- positive firm-profit mass
- household wage income
- transfers
- household net income
- equity primary issuance
- household portfolio ownership
- public share ratio
- settlement entries whose kind indicates dividends or profit distribution

Hypothesis BG-1: firm ownership and profit-to-household income recycling are too sparse to form a mature circular-flow channel.

Absence of a dividend-like settlement is a structural finding, not by itself proof of collapse causality.

## R4-BH — Capital lifecycle / depreciation / stranded capital

Measure:
- firm-level positive and negative capital-stock changes
- total gross investment
- total capital additions and reductions
- active vs inactive/stranded capital stock
- entrant capital acquisition
- capital reduction event frequency

Hypothesis BH-1: capital is largely one-way accumulating, while exited-firm capital remains stranded rather than depreciating, being liquidated, or reallocated.

If confirmed, this may bias capacity upward and therefore cannot automatically be called a collapse cause.

## R4-BI — Banking architecture / credit concentration

Measure:
- number of commercial banks and central banks
- unique bank IDs used by firms and households
- credit applications / approvals / originations / defaults
- firm vs household origination shares
- approval rate
- active-loan stock

Hypothesis BI-1: one-bank-per-country architecture creates complete institutional concentration and may suppress realistic diversification/reallocation channels.

This is an architecture finding unless a later causal ablation demonstrates macro importance.

## R4-BJ — Labor reallocation topology

Track household employer transitions:
- incumbent job -> unemployment
- unemployment -> job
- direct job-to-job employer switch
- hires into entrant firms
- hires into incumbents

Hypothesis BJ-1: labor reallocation is dominated by unemployment-mediated separation/re-entry rather than direct job-to-job mobility, slowing ecological adaptation.

## R4-BK — Price/wage adjustment asymmetry

Track firm-level monthly changes:
- price change frequency and absolute magnitude
- wage change frequency and absolute magnitude
- wage/consumer-price ratio path

Hypothesis BK-1: prices adjust while nominal firm wages are effectively fixed or much more rigid, causing relative labor-cost adjustment to occur through employment/exit instead of wage bargaining.

## Network side-channel

For no extra simulation cost, record interfirm purchase pairs and repeat-pair shares to preserve supply-network maturity evidence.
