## Stock-sufficient shortage

In the NONCAPITAL baseline:

- raw-material supplier stock is sufficient for aggregate net need in **95.1%** of country-product months;
- **90.8% of raw-material shortage units** occur during those stock-sufficient months;
- processed-material stock is sufficient in **91.0%** of country-product months;
- **51.5% of processed-material shortage units** occur during those stock-sufficient months.

RESOURCE+CONSUMER is even more direct for raw material: aggregate stock is sufficient in 100% of observed country-product months, yet procurement still leaves shortage; all of that shortage therefore occurs despite aggregate stock sufficiency.

This does not mean every individual buyer could necessarily purchase every unit at its actual cash/price/search state. It means the economy-wide shortage cannot generally be attributed to a lack of physical supplier inventory.
