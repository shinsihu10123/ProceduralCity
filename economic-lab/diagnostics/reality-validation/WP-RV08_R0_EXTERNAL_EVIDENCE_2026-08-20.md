# WP-RV08-R0 — External Evidence Baseline

Date: 2026-08-20
Status: **PASS — STRUCTURAL EVIDENCE BASELINE FROZEN FOR REPAIR DESIGN**

## Purpose

This note freezes external principles that constrain repair design. It is not a calibration table and does not authorize fitted coefficients.

## Authoritative sources reviewed

1. **United Nations — System of National Accounts 2008 / SNA concepts in brief**
   - National accounts require a coherent, integrated production/income/expenditure accounting framework.
   - Finished goods transferred into inventories are valued consistently with the value of output at the time of transfer.
   - Changes in inventories are additions less withdrawals and regular losses; they are not an arbitrary residual generated by payroll alone.

2. **OECD — Financing SMEs and Entrepreneurs 2026**
   - SME finance is not limited to unsecured bank loans; asset-based finance can use receivables, inventories, machinery, equipment and other assets.
   - Credit conditions, bankruptcies, non-performing loans and alternative finance all matter for firm entry and survival.

3. **OECD — Strengthening SMEs and Entrepreneurship for Productivity and Inclusive Growth**
   - Appropriate finance matters across the business life cycle, including start-up, development and growth.
   - Financing constraints can impair post-entry performance, investment, productivity and employment.

4. **OECD — Policy Lessons from Financing Innovative Firms**
   - Young firms can face a distinct seed/early-stage financing gap; start-up finance need not be modeled as ordinary mature-firm debt underwriting only.

## Repair-design implications

### E1 — Inventory accounting

A repair must not create finished-goods inventory book value solely from wage accrual when no physical output is produced. Physical additions, valuation and cost recognition must be tied to an explicit production-accounting event.

### E2 — Production and income accounts

Compensation of employees is an income/cost flow associated with production, but the production account must separately identify output and intermediate consumption. A valid repair should eventually support independent expenditure/production/income reconciliation rather than defining GDP through one residual bookkeeping path.

### E3 — Working capital

Working-capital finance should correspond to current production/payroll/input financing needs. Using a field reset to zero before the current production plan exists is not economically interpretable as a current financing requirement.

### E4 — Entrant finance

A replacement entrant should not be assumed to have the same information, collateral, operating history and financing contract as a mature distressed borrower. Conversely, the model must not silently gift money. The source, instrument, liability/equity treatment and risk-bearing party must be explicit.

### E5 — Multiple financing instruments

A production-ready entrant design may use debt, equity, asset-backed finance, trade credit or another explicit financing instrument, but the implementation must preserve the ledger and accounting identities and must not be selected because it hits a target unemployment rate.

## Non-calibration rule

No empirical coefficient is selected in R0. External sources constrain semantics and accounting architecture only. Numeric calibration is deferred until a structurally coherent candidate survives regression and held-out validation.
