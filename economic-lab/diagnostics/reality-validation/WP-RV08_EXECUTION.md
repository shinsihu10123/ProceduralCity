## R1 — current-plan working-capital timing

R1 is the first experimental repair isolation.

Variants under the existing P2 nominal unit-basis development environment:

1. `control` — canonical credit timing and canonical `supplyShortage`-based input-need application.
2. `provisional-current-input` — keep canonical credit timing, but derive input financing need from the current firm decision and a provisional production plan using `desiredWorkers`, current inventories and current supplier prices.
3. `postplan-exact-input` — defer credit until after labor clearing and `planProduction`, then derive input financing need from current exact `desiredProduction` before procurement.

No loan approval threshold, risk limit, affordability limit, bank capital floor, payroll coefficient or macro target is altered.
