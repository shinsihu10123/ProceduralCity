## Why it is not the dominant explanation

1. The intervention does not remove the broader-variant employment gap. MATERIALS+CONSUMER with the cap still has FULL unemployment `0.2175`, slightly above CONSUMER control `0.2161`; NONCAPITAL remains much higher at `0.2296`.
2. FULL negative-hiring share does not fall. It rises under the cap because later endogenous states change, even while the defense share generally falls.
3. Physical output changes are modest and mixed; the intervention primarily changes decision propagation rather than removing the underlying production/cash structure.
4. RESOURCE+CONSUMER shows a late M10-12 unemployment deterioration (`+0.292 pp` within its pair), demonstrating that the inventory signal is not a one-direction global lever.
