## 5. Decision-history hardening

v0.10 uses `compact-v2` for historical decision records while retaining the full current `lastReasoning` trace.

The storage split is intentional:

- **current decision:** full reasoning detail for inspection/debugging
- **historical decisions:** compact audit record
- **learning state:** beliefs, forecasts, episodic memory, causal model, strategy reward state remain separate and active

## 7. Multi-shock stress matrix

The audited stress matrix runs paired control/treatment worlds across two seeds and tests four distinct intervention channels:

- productivity supply shock
- firm demand-expectation belief shock
- bank risk-aversion shock
- tariff-friction shock

For every scenario:

- the configured direct state moves in the configured direction;
- the intervention is applied exactly once;
- treated and control worlds remain healthy;
- the treated AST economy produces a non-zero endogenous macro response;
- scenario response signatures are pairwise distinct.

The gate deliberately does **not** prescribe that GDP, inflation, unemployment, or defaults must move in a pre-authored direction. Those variables remain endogenous outcomes.

## 8. Emergence ensemble

The 24-month emergence ensemble uses three seeds plus an exact repeat of the first seed. Its audited gates require:

- all runs healthy;
- all emergence metrics finite;
- non-degenerate macro dynamics in every country;
- exact reproduction for the repeated seed;
- distinct macro paths across different seeds.

All gates passed. Pairwise seed-signature L1 distances were non-zero (`21.03`, `27.10`, `24.50` in the audited run), while the repeated same-seed report was exactly equal.

## 9. Accounting and long-run stability

## 10. Known limitations / remaining performance debt

v0.10 closeout does **not** claim that x10 is production-scale real-time simulation. Current known debt includes:

- high x10 retained cognition state and V8 heap pressure;
- garbage collection remaining a major CPU component;
- per-agent causal models and episodic memory being intentionally rich but memory-expensive;
- General Ledger journal retention becoming non-trivial at large scale;
- steady-state x10 being substantially slower than cold month 1 because more learning/history state is active;
- benchmark times varying across GitHub-hosted runners.

These items are explicitly carried forward as performance-engineering evidence. They are not hidden by lowering correctness gates or reducing the economic model.
