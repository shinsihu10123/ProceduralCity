## Implemented boundary

The registry records semantic authority identity/reference metadata for canonical facts without taking over future WP-002 world-entity identity or PA-043 binary serialization decisions.

Implemented behavior:

1. One canonical fact can have only one registered semantic owner.
2. Authority record identity is namespace + local ID and cannot be duplicated or reused after retirement/tombstone.
3. Namespace ownership cannot silently move to another semantic owner.
4. Registration consumes the validated `S1.01.01` receipt and verifies fact, owner, writer, state class, version and causal parent rather than repairing mismatches.
5. Exact-version references reject dangling or stale references.
6. Authority epoch updates require the current owner and a strictly advancing epoch.
7. Active, inactive and tombstone lifecycle states are distinct; create/update/retire/tombstone lineage is retained.
8. Derived/Observer/Renderer/Analytics access is represented only as read-only role metadata and cannot become canonical owner authority.
9. Snapshot/restore preserves ID, owner, version, lifecycle, read-only access, lineage and deterministic Evidence digest without selecting the future canonical binary format.
10. Same operation sequence produces identical reference state, lineage order and Evidence digest.
