## Frozen behavior implemented

- Records Fact ID, stable Authority ID/namespace, exact Authority version, Owner, allowed Writer, Authority Epoch, lifecycle state, parent version and causal reference.
- Preserves create/update/retire/tombstone Authority lineage from the Canonical Authority Registry.
- Uses a stable Manifest identity and monotonically versioned Manifest lineage.
- Validates manifest schema version, required source hash, actor owner, write origin and causal parent before producing a result.
- Distinguishes Active, Inactive and Tombstone authority targets; retired Authority IDs are never reinterpreted as new targets.
- Rejects duplicate Fact mappings, duplicate Authority identity mappings, dangling references and stale references.
- Manifest replacement validates compatibility and forward migration before mutation; failed replacement leaves the previous manifest unchanged.
- Only the registered Authority Manifest owner through the Authority Registry write origin may replace the Manifest. Derived, Observer, Renderer and Analytics writes are blocked.
- Snapshot/restore and registry-backed load preserve exact identity, versions, lifecycle, lineage and Evidence digest.
- Replay with the same registry snapshot, schema, source hash and causal reference is deterministic.
