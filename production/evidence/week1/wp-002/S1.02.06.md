## Frozen acceptance basis

The FINAL v2.1.3a schedule defines S1.02.06 as the `persistence` operation for the operands `Tombstone`, `Historical`, `Retention`, `Stable`, and `Entity`. It reads the Frozen WHAT/HOW root contract plus stable ID, namespace, version and lifecycle lineage. It must verify a commit marker, causal cut, schema version and required state before recording a validated durable artifact, recovery position, parent cut and replay reference.

The function-specific failure oracle requires partial save, corrupt segment, old schema and references outside the cut to fail rather than treating data after the last valid recovery point as valid. The function-specific boundary requires pending pre-commit state to remain distinct from durable post-marker evidence. Wrong-owner and Derived/Observer/Renderer write paths must not become authoritative. Persistence and replay must preserve ID, version, pending/causal evidence and digest.

This L3 remains under the Frozen invariant Candidate != Reality. The retention processor validates durable retention evidence but does not itself perform Canonical Commit or invent later cross-reference, snapshot/reload or identity-reuse semantics.
